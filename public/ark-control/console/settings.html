Identity and access

Operator identity is not authenticated in this deployment. You are acting as operations@arctransformationgroup.com because a request header says so, behind a single shared token. Anyone holding that token can claim any identity, so an approval recorded here establishes that the workflow ran — not who ran it. Set ARC_OIDC_ISSUER to sign operators in against your identity provider; every approval cast after that is attributable to a person.
