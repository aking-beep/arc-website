# ARC AI Product Playbook

## From Idea to Shipped Product

A working method for turning something you built with AI into something people
can actually use.

<div class="front-note">

Published by ARC Transformation Group.

ARC helps people and organizations understand, adopt, build and operate the
technologies shaping modern systems. This Playbook is the product-development
sequence we run on paid work, written down so you can run it yourself.

</div>

<div class="page-break"></div>

## What this is

Eight modules and eleven worksheets, in the order the decisions actually
happen. Each module names one decision, explains why it is cheaper to make at
that point than later, gives you the method, and tells you what you should be
holding when you are done.

## What this is not

It is not a course. There are no videos, no certificate and nothing to log
into.

It is not a code template. You will not find a repository to clone. Every
decision in here is one a model cannot make for you, because it depends on who
your customer is and what you are willing to be wrong about.

It is not a survey of AI tooling. The method deliberately avoids naming
specific models and vendors, because those change faster than this document
can, and the sequence does not depend on them.

## What it costs you

Working time. The modules are short on purpose; the worksheets are where the
effort goes. If you fill in every worksheet honestly you will spend a day or
two, and you will know more about your product than you did.

If you read it without filling anything in, it will feel obvious and change
nothing. That is a fair warning rather than a sales tactic.
