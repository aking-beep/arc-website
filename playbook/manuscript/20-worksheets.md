# Worksheets

Eleven pages. Each one belongs to a module and produces one of its outputs.

They are here twice: printed, to write on, and in the ZIP as editable Markdown
with the same field names, so a team can keep them in the repository alongside
the thing they describe. Filling these in is the work. The modules explain why
each field is the one being asked for.

<div class="worksheet" data-module="01">

## Worksheet 1 — Product hypothesis

*Module 01 · Idea*

One sentence with four specific slots. A category in any slot means it is not
specific yet.

<div class="field" data-lines="1">For (a specific person — someone you could name)</div>
<div class="field" data-lines="2">Who (has this specific problem)</div>
<div class="field" data-lines="2">This gives them (this specific outcome)</div>
<div class="field" data-lines="2">Instead of (what they actually do today)</div>
